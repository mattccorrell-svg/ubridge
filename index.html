Your study data stays on your computer

  • Runs locally. No account, no analytics and no study data sent elsewhere.
  • Question IDs only. Never reads or copies UWorld questions, answers or explanations.
  • Your UWorld password stays with UWorld. Sign in directly on UWorld’s page.
  • You’re in control. Cards never change without a click, and UBridge never grades them.
What UBridge and its Chrome extension (coming soon) read and store

When you click it, the extension reads question IDs and whether each was right, wrong or marked. It also does this when you open the results of a test you haven’t logged yet, so the test logs itself (Log by itself, on by default; turn it off in the extension’s settings). To identify logged tests, it also reads each test’s name, ID and date, plus the UWorld subscription number in the page URL, from UWorld’s results and Previous Tests pages. It never stores or sends question text, answers or explanations, and communicates only with Anki on your computer.

Button settings are stored in Chrome and may sync with your Google account if Chrome sync is enabled. Chrome also stores locally: recent UWorld test names and dates, so sent tests retain their dates; logged-test records, so status dots remain visible when Anki is closed (set Logged marks to Off in the popup to delete them); and your UWorld course URL. Question IDs are not stored.

The connection requires enabling Connect the UBridge Chrome extension in UBridge’s Settings. It listens only on 127.0.0.1, responds only to the UBridge extension and cannot add, edit or delete cards.

In UBridge, Question history is saved locally. The optional UWorld window retains your UWorld sign-in on this computer. On shared computers, use UWorld in your regular browser instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is UBridge made by UWorld or AnKing?

No. UBridge is an independent tool built by a med student, not affiliated with or endorsed by UWorld, AnKing or Anki. It uses only the UWorld question IDs already tagged in your deck.

Does UBridge read UWorld questions?

No. The add-on never reads, copies or stores UWorld questions, answers or explanations. It uses only question IDs from your cards’ tags. The Chrome extension (coming soon) reads question IDs and whether each was right, wrong or marked, never question text. See Privacy for everything it reads and stores.

Which decks and exams are supported?

Any deck with notes tagged by UWorld question ID. AnKing’s UWorld tags for Step 1, Step 2 CK, Step 3, COMLEX and MCAT work without setup, including v11 and v12 Step decks. Using different tags? Configure them in Settings →to Advanced options. UWorld only; no AMBOSS or NBME support. Runs in desktop Anki, not AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid or AnkiWeb. Nothing appears during card reviews.
