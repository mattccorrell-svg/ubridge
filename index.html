Your studying stays on your computer

  • Runs on your computer. No account, no analytics, no study data sent anywhere.
  • Question IDs only. It never reads or copies UWorld questions, answers or explanations.
  • Your UWorld password stays with UWorld. You sign in on UWorld’s own page.
  • A locked-down Chrome link. Off until you turn it on, 127.0.0.1 only, and it can’t change cards.
  • Nothing behind your back. It never changes cards without a click, or grades them for you.
Everything the Chrome extension reads and keeps

When you click, it reads question IDs and whether each was right, wrong or marked. To show which tests you’ve logged, it also reads each test’s name, ID and date, and the UWorld subscription number in the page’s address, on UWorld’s results and Previous Tests pages. It never keeps or sends question text, answers or explanations, and it talks only to Anki on your computer.

It keeps its button settings in Chrome’s own storage, which Chrome can sync with your Google account if you use Chrome sync. It also keeps, in Chrome on this computer: the names and dates of your recent UWorld tests, so a test you send arrives dated; a list of which tests are logged, so the dots still show while Anki is closed (setting Logged marks to Off in its popup deletes it); and your UWorld course’s page address. It doesn’t store question IDs.

The link only works after you turn on Connect the UBridge Chrome extension in UBridge’s Settings. It listens only on 127.0.0.1, answers only the UBridge extension, and can’t add, change or delete cards.

In the add-on: your Question history is saved in a file on your computer. The optional UWorld window keeps your UWorld sign-in on this computer so you stay logged in, so on a shared computer, use UWorld in your normal browser instead.

UBridge for Chrome

Optional. One click from UWorld results to Anki.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

You don’t need it to use UBridge.

Add to Chrome
  1. Add UBridge to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. In Anki, open UBridge →to Settings… and turn on Connect the UBridge Chrome extension.
  3. With Anki open, click the UBridge icon in Chrome. It should say Connected to Anki.

Not connecting? Update UBridge in Anki with Tools →to Add-ons →to Check for Updates.

Not connecting? Download UBridge again, install it the same way, then restart Anki.

What you need

You need Anki 2.1.50 or newer on Mac or Windows (Linux should work, not yet tested), a deck with UWorld ID tags like the AnKing decks (v11 and v12), and your own UWorld subscription.

The Chrome extension needs Chrome 102 or newer.

Common questions

Is this made by UWorld or AnKing?

No. UBridge is an independent tool made by a med student. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by UWorld, AnKing or Anki. It only uses the UWorld question IDs already in your deck’s tags.

Does it read UWorld questions?

No. The add-on never reads, copies or stores UWorld questions, answers or explanations. It works only with question IDs, the numbers in your cards’ tags. The Chrome extension reads question IDs and whether each was right, wrong or marked, never question text. Privacy lists everything it reads and keeps.

Which decks and exams work?

Any deck whose notes are tagged with UWorld question IDs. AnKing’s UWorld tags for Step 1, Step 2 CK, Step 3, COMLEX and MCAT work with no setup, including the v11 and v12 Step decks. If your deck is tagged another way, you can set its tag format in Settings →to Advanced options. NBME practice exams and other question banks aren’t supported.

Will it repeat questions I’ve done?

Not once UBridge knows you’ve done them. Blocks you copy with Copy & mark used go into your Question history. So do tests you send from the Chrome extension and, unless you turn it off, questions you look up in Find Cards. New blocks skip all of them. Did some UWorld before installing? Add those tests once in Question history by pasting their question IDs, or click Log on UBridge on each test’s results page.

Does my Question history sync between computers?

No. It’s saved on your computer, and it survives add-on updates and new AnKing deck versions. To move it, use Settings →to Export… on one computer and Import… on the other. Uninstalling the add-on deletes it, so export first. Changes to your cards, like unsuspending or tags, are normal Anki changes and sync as usual.

Will it change my cards?

Only when you click. Unsuspend in Browser doesn’t change anything by itself: it shows just the suspended cards in Anki’s Browser, selected, so you can look them over and unsuspend them there (Ctrl+J, ⌘J on a Mac). Make a review deck, Add a tag and Unsuspend and study these first change cards when you click them. They’re normal Anki changes, and Edit →to Undo reverts each one. It never grades or reviews cards for you.

What doesn’t it do?

It’s UWorld only, with no AMBOSS or NBME. It runs in desktop Anki, not AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid or AnkiWeb, and it doesn’t show anything while you review cards. Your cards need UWorld question-ID tags, like the AnKing decks have. It can’t see your UWorld results by itself: you paste the IDs, or click the Chrome extension. And it’s new, at version 1.0, so there are no AnkiWeb ratings or reviews to go on yet.
