How UBridge was compared

In October 2026, I searched AnkiWeb for “uworld” and read the listings of the 25 UWorld-related add-ons it found, and installed 4 of them to read their code. Listings change, so this is a snapshot of what they said then. — Matt, maker of UBridge

  • 13 UWorld →to Anki
  • 9 Anki →to UWorld
  • 3 Both ways

Direction is what each listing describes. UWorld →to Anki: from UWorld question IDs to your Anki cards. Anki →to UWorld: from your cards to UWorld question IDs.

Chrome extension says Yes or No where the listing or code says so, and is left blank where it wasn’t stated.

UWqidAS’s AnkiWeb page stands in for an add-on offered to AnKing members. Its direction comes from that add-on’s code.
